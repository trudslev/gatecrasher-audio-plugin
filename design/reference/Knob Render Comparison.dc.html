RECOMMENDATION
Filmstrips at @1x and @2x for the four knob sizes. The sculpted metal is the whole point of this panel — it is what separates a 1985 rack unit from a generic plugin, and column A shows how much of that is lost to arcs. Ship @2x strips and the blur above disappears at every realistic window scale. If the plugin needs free resizing rather than fixed steps, invert this: code-drawn knobs, and accept the flatter read.
