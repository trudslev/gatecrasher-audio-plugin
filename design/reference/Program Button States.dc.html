The legend itself lights. Lit #F4F8FA with a 7px bloom; unlit #9AA1A6, no bloom. Both legends are permanently printed at 10px / 600 / .10em and neither moves, changes weight or changes size — the backlight is the only variable.
These two caps are dark because of it. On the pale fascia a bright legend has nowhere brighter to go, so the cap drops to #23282C → #14181B and the legend reads as illuminated against it. Lit measures 15.9:1 on the cap, unlit 5.2:1 — well clear of the 3:1 state floor, so both dark reads as “nothing to do here”, never as a blank button. No disabled face: cap, border and inner highlight are identical in all five states.
